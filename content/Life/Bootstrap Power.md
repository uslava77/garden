# Habit
Most of my life is on a habit loop. 
Day-day, week-week, what I do and when I do it follows a pattern.
# Goals
Most 'goals' require sustained effort over time.

It is useful to align habits with goals.
**Because even if an activity requires effort, habits prevent you from *not* doing it.**
# Bootstrap
1. Pick a habit to stop or start
2. Direct all energy and resources into this change
    1. Habit hack resources help
3. Persist until successful
# Meta-Loop
When you succeed, pat yourself on the back, shit's wasn't easy.

Repeat and collect receipts as mounting evidence to yourself that you have power to change yourself.
