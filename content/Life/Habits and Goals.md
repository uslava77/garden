Most of my life is on a habit loop. 
Day-day, week-week, what I do and when I do it follows a pattern.

It is useful to align habits with goals, because:
- Most 'goals' require sustained effort over time
- **Even if an activity requires effort, habits prevent you from *not* doing it**
# It's a skill
Habit change is tough (by definition), but not impossible.
It is a skill that can be developed.

Skill acquisition is composed of:
1. Learning
2. Practice
# Learning
Relevant readings:
- Habit hacking
- Behavior Modification
# Practice
To succeed we must scale.

The first step should be small:
- ex. stretch while in elevator

The goal is to:
- **Apply techniques from [Learning](#Learning), and reflect on what's working
- Build confidence in your own capacity to change

It should feel rewarding and empowering to successfully eliminate misaligned habits.
You may even build a habit of changing habits.
# Experimentation
Alignment caveats:
- It may not be clear if a habit is aligned or not.
- It may be aligned initially, but misaligned after some time
- Your judgement of alignment may be wrong

It is necessary to maintain an 'always a student' mindset and experiment.
