Summarized thoughts on free will and illusion of choice.
# Physics Argument
As far as we can tell, the world evolves through time according to laws of physics.

## Me
Upon reflection, I seem to exist in the same world that behaves according to physics.
We can inspect all parts of a human and confirm it is made of the same substance as everything else.

## Prediction
If I am made of stuff that evolves through time according to physics, than the same is true for my entire self.

In short if we have:
1. Knowledge of physical laws
2. Knowledge of my state
3. Sufficient compute

We could calculate my thoughts, actions, body functions, etc.

# Biology Argument
As far as we can tell, human bodily function is a product of genes and environment.
## Genes
I did not pick my parents, therefore I had no say in what genes I got.

## Environment
When I was born, I also did not pick my environment.
In fact, I probably had no say in the matter for the first few years.

## Thoughts
My thoughts come mostly from my brain which is part of my body and also subject to genes, environment, physics.

Any choices made after the first few years are a by-product of the previous years, where we had no choice.

Thus 'choices' made in the past are an illusion, they were simply a result of interactions of the previous state, genes, the environment, physics.

# Bound by Flesh
It's quite annoying when logical arguments clash with our intuition/feelings.

I accept that I am governed by processes outside my 'control'.

Yet I also accept that I am stuck in this body, the illusion of choice is all I have.
