# Familiarity
Full appreciation for an innovation necessitates familiarity with the:
1. base movement
2. history of innovations

Example:
- Knowing what a windmill looks like, we can understand what a variation looks like.
- Knowing the history of windmill variations, we can detect when one is *new*.
