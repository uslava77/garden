At any of the [Move, Combo, Set](Definitions.md#Move,%20Combo,%20Set) levels, we have a choice to:
- A) plan what we will do ahead of time ([Choreography](Definitions.md#Choreography))
- B) make the decisions in the moment ([Freestyle](Definitions.md#Freestyle))
- C) some combination of the two

We'll discuss the benefits, disadvantage, and struggles of each approach.

# ROUND LEVEL
## Choreo
In this case, we plan move-for-move exactly what the whole round start-finish will look like.

With almost unlimited time to *think* about what combos to execute, we have the advantage of optimizing the round to fulfill [certain qualities](Round%20Qualities.md) like dynamics, length, or just ensure execution of a specific combo.

Within the context of cyphers and battles, this is ill-advised.

You do not know what:
- Music will play
- Your battle opponent will do
- Your physical and emotional state will be
- The environment will be (floor, space)
- Will not go according to plan

Breaking is a freestyle dance, the essence is in using all of the above to express yourself. 
## Freestyle
At the round level, freestyle is deciding which combos to execute either:
1. right before you're about to go in
2. mid-round
3. or some combination of both

This opens up the possibility to respond to others, scale the length of rounds, adapt to surroundings, and some ability to respond to music.

However, if our combos have no way to connect, we will face [The Continuity Struggle](Dance%20is%20Continuous.md#The%20Continuity%20Struggle). 
To freestyle at the round-level successfully we must build out the [Combo Graph](Combo%20Graph.md).

Additionally, with new-found options we face Analysis Paralysis. Now that we can traverse the combo graph, which path do we walk? 
If we want to move beyond random decision-making, we must 
- Random
- Response to music, opponent, environment, self
- Self-imposed constraint/game

## Roadmap
While choreo is best left for theater performances, it is still useful to consider the [Round Qualities](Round%20Qualities.md) you want to achieve. This can be used as a roadmap for decision-making in the moment and alleviate [Analysis Paralysis](#Analysis%20Paralysis).

This approach combines planning ahead, with decision making in the moment.
