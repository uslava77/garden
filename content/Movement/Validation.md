# Internal
You've likely heard an 'OG' say something like:

> You have to dance for you, and not for anybody else

Here's my take on why I think there's a correlation between **years** and **relying on internal validation**:
1. If it's fun, we stay in the game. 
2. If it's not fun more often than it is fun, we will likely quit.
3. Most will lose much much more than win

If there isn't some internal validation going on to push through inevitable times where you no longer succeed in competitions, then you end up quitting.

"Doing it for you" is about **maximizing personal enjoyment of the [time you spend](Value%20of%20Time.md) on the craft**. 

# External
Realistically, "doing it for you" is only possible in a vacuum.

Humans are social. We have a built-in need to connect.

Social connection is how dance came to be in the first place.
If you weren't exposed to dance through others, you wouldn't even know it exists.

Thus, there's a part of us that inevitably wants to share the thing we worked on so hard.
