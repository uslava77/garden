
# The Continuity Struggle
*"The End is Just The Beginning"*

Every combo leads us into a **new position from which we must have a transition into the next one**.  The end of one combo, is the beginning of new movement. This is the **continuity struggle**.

Symptoms:
- Awkward movement to find a way into the next combo, "searching"
- Round cut short
# Chopping
While a 6-step is a 'move', it actually contains many sub-moves, like hooks and sweeps, which may be considered 'moves'. We *could* claim that a 6-step is a combo.  

In general, because **time is continuous**, we can split **any** movement into **infinite** chunks. This means a move to one person, could be a combo to another.  
Similarly, a set can be combined with another set, and we can re-interpret each to be a combo.

There is no way to make an objective distinction between a move, combo, or a set.  
It is up to the individual to define how "long" a move, combo, set is.
